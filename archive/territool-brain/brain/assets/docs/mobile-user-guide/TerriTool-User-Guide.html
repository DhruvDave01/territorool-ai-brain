Page 5

AI Find Leads

What this feature does

AI Find Leads finds businesses that match what you sell. You search on a map, pick companies, and save them to your account. This uses credits from your plan. Best workflow: Find leads → Build a Route → Find Contacts at each stop.

🗺 MAP — pins show companies
AI Find Leads
Describe companies you want…
Search
  1. Tap AI Find Leads (Home tile or bottom icon).
  2. Allow Location if asked — map centers on you.
  3. Type what you want (example: "plumbers in Dallas") and tap Search.
  4. Tap pins on the map → select companies → Save.
  5. Next: turn saved companies into a Route (Page 6).
"Insufficient credits" — go to Settings (Page 12) to upgrade or ask your manager.

Page 6

Routes — Plan Your Driving Day

What this feature does

A Route is an ordered list of companies to visit in one trip. TerriTool optimizes the order to save driving time. Use routes when you are in the field: open a stop → Navigate → visit → Mark Visited → next stop.

Create a route

  1. Tap Routes → Create Route.
  2. Name it (example: "Friday AM").
  3. Add companies. Set start/end on map if asked.
  4. Tap Save / publish when done.

Run your route

  1. Open the route → tap first company.
  2. Tap Navigate → maps open for directions.
  3. After the visit tap Mark Visited.
  4. Use 1/7 arrows at top to go to next company.

Page 7

Lists — Group Your Companies

What this feature does

Lists are folders for companies. Example: "Hot Prospects" or "Trade Show Leads." Lists work like routes — open the list and work through companies one by one. Use lists when you want a custom group that is not tied to a map route.

Lists

Your company groups

Hot Prospects
12 companies
+ Create List
  1. Tap Lists on bottom menu.
  2. Tap Create List and type a name.
  3. Add companies from your Address Book or from AI Find Leads.
  4. Open the list anytime to work through those companies.
Routes vs Lists: Routes = driving order on a map. Lists = grouped companies without map optimization. Many reps use both.

Page 9

Add Activity — Notes & Follow-Ups

What this feature does

Add Activity records what happened at a company (or what you plan to do later). Examples: "Left voicemail," "Met with owner," "Send quote Friday." You pick a date (required), time (optional), and an activity type. Future dates show button Schedule; today/past show Save.

  1. On company page tap + Add Activity.
  2. Type a Note — what happened or your plan.
  3. Pick Activity type: Meeting, Call, Visit, Message, Email, Reminder, or Appointment.
  4. Tap Date → use < > arrows to change month → tap a day.
  5. Time (optional) — tap to set time, or tap Clear to skip time.
  6. Tap Save or Schedule.
Past date? App asks "Are you sure?" — tap Continue to log yesterday's visit.
Edit later: Tap an activity in the list → change fields → Save or Delete.

Page 10

Find Contacts

What this feature does

Find Contacts searches the internet and business databases for decision-makers at a company — names, titles, emails, phone numbers. This uses credits. Results appear in the Contacts section on the company page.

ABC Roofing LLC
Contacts (0)
Find Contacts
or Add Contact manually
  1. Open a company page.
  2. Scroll to Contacts.
  3. Tap the blue Find Contacts button.
  4. Wait — search can take 30–60 seconds.
  5. When contacts appear, tap to Call, email, or save.
No results? Not every company has public data. Tap Add Contact to type someone in manually.

Page 11

Scan Business Card

What this feature does

Scan Card uses your phone camera and AI to read a business card. It pulls out the name, company, phone, and email — then saves them to your account. Great after trade shows or networking events.

  5. Tap Save — contact + company added.

What this feature does

Settings (gear icon) shows your profile, credit balance, and subscription status. Credits are used for AI Find Leads, Find Contacts, and Scan Card. When credits run out or subscription expires, some features stop until you renew.
