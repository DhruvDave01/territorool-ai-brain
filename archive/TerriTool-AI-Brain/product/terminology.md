# Terminology

Canonical product language: [glossary.md](glossary.md).

Use those words in PRs, Brain updates, and MCP replies. Do not invent synonyms for:

- Company / Contact / Note / Activity / Visit
- List vs Route
- Credits vs subscription / plan (`user_packages`)
- Find Leads vs add company
- Find contacts vs buy / redeem contacts
- Sales Agent, Business Sales Agent, Business Agent, Admin

Concepts with implementation pointers: [concepts.md](concepts.md).
