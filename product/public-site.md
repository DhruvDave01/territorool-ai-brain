## What the site says the product does

One AI platform for field sales. It finds leads in a territory, finds a decision-maker’s phone and email, and plans the route. The field app hands off to Apple Maps or CarPlay, and visits and notes sync back.

Four steps named on the site:

1. **AI Find Leads** — businesses in the territory matched to who the rep sells to.
2. **AI Contact Finder** — verified email and mobile number.
3. **Patented AI Routes** — stop order for the day.
4. **Field-ready mobile app** — start and end points, log the visit, sync the CRM.

Named CRMs: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive. The site says TerriTool is not a CRM. It feeds the CRM.

Industries named: solar, pest control, home services, telecom, security, logistics, industrial supply — any face-to-face sale to businesses.

## Do not copy into the product app

Marketing lines on the site (savings, “4× your territory”, testimonial percentages, “set up in 90 seconds”) are not product rules. Do not put them in the app as facts.

The site’s “2 credits per phone or email” is the public pricing sentence. In the app, the live costs are still the table in [overview.md](overview.md): search 0, save an AI lead 1, buy a contact 2 or 1 on the Claude/biz path.
